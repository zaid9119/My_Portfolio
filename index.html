FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do you need from me to create a thumbnail?

Send the video title or topic, a short explanation of the video, any photos you want included and a few style references if you have them.

Do you design only YouTube thumbnails?

My main focus is YouTube thumbnail design. I can also help with a consistent thumbnail style for your channel.

How long does a thumbnail take?

Timing depends on the brief and complexity. Share your deadline when you contact me and I'll confirm a realistic delivery time.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. We can discuss the revision scope before starting so expectations are clear.

Which tools do you use?

I use design tools including Adobe Photoshop and Canva, choosing the workflow that best fits the project.
